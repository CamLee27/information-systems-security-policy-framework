# SWOT Analysis

## Context

This SWOT analysis was created for **Swift**, a fictional student transportation platform connecting students, parents, drivers, schools, and a company application.

The analysis was part of the original Information Systems Security Policy project.

## Strength: Data Encryption

A major proposed strength was the use of encryption to protect sensitive information.

Encryption would help reduce the impact of unauthorized access by making protected information unreadable without the appropriate access or keys. It could also support user confidence in the organization’s handling of student, parent, school, and transportation information.

## Weakness: Human Error and Security Awareness

The project identified users as a major weakness.

Students, parents, employees, drivers, and other users may have different levels of cybersecurity knowledge. Human error, weak security practices, and accidental disclosure could contribute to unauthorized access or data breaches.

The proposed response included:

- Security-awareness training
- Clear educational materials
- Incident-reporting guidance
- Strong account-security requirements
- Ongoing communication about threats and safe practices

## Opportunity: Analytics and Route Optimization

The project identified additional analytics as an opportunity.

Potential uses included:

- Route optimization
- Improved service quality
- Reduced fuel costs
- Reduced travel time
- Identification of operational patterns
- Better decision-making

These benefits would require appropriate privacy, access-control, and data-governance protections.

## Threat: External Attackers and Social Engineering

The project identified external attackers as a major threat.

Attackers could target technical vulnerabilities or users with limited security awareness. The proposed protections included:

- Firewalls
- Intrusion detection
- Monitoring
- Updates and patching
- Incident-response planning
- User training and awareness

## Summary Table

| Category | Original Finding | Proposed Response |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | Encryption protects sensitive data | Encrypt information and restrict access |
| Weakness | Human error and limited awareness | Training, guidance, and account controls |
| Opportunity | Analytics and route optimization | Use data to improve efficiency with proper governance |
| Threat | Attackers may target systems or users | Monitoring, firewalls, updates, response plans, and awareness |
